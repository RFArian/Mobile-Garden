How repairs work

  1. Book

    Choose your repair and a day online, or walk in during opening hours.

  2. We check and quote

    We look at your phone and confirm the final price before any work starts.

  3. We repair it

    Simple repairs are often done the same day. We call you when it is ready.

  4. Test and collect

    Check the phone with us when you collect it. Your repair warranty covers it from that day.

Repair questions

How long does a repair take?
The time is shown next to each repair above. Screen and battery changes are often finished the same day. Water damage checks can take a day or two. When we call to confirm your booking we tell you the exact time for your phone.
Is my data safe?
We do not need to look at your photos, messages or files. Most repairs such as a screen or battery change do not touch your data, but no repair is completely risk free, so please back up your phone before you bring it in. If a repair could ever need a reset, we tell you first.
Do I need to pay before the repair?
No. We confirm the final price before we start, and you pay when you collect your phone.
